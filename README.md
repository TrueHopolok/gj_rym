<h3>Description</h3>
<p>
</p>
<p>Destined for a higher plane of being, a Pharaoh awaits his judgement, basking in the golden rays of Osiris.
</p>
<p>But then, something drags him back to the mortal plane. Was that a mistake? How can this be? Maybe finding his sarcophagus would let him into the heavens once more?
